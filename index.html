TRAVEL WITH MORE AWARENESS

Small checks. Better journeys.

Safe Route combines practical navigation with useful safety habits. It does not replace your judgement or emergency services.

01

Before you leave

Check the route, charge your phone, share your itinerary and keep a backup way to contact someone.

02

While travelling

Prefer well-lit places to stop, avoid distracted driving and keep emergency contacts easy to reach.

03

When plans change

Re-check conditions, choose a public location and contact local emergency services when there is immediate danger.
